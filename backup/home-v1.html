Our story

From Dubai's dessert craze to your street

Go Laban started with one idea: the creamy, layered Egyptian-style desserts everyone was queuing for in the UAE shouldn't need a flight to Dubai.

We brought the recipes, the Nutella, Lotus and pistachio combinations and the kunafa crunch home to Madurai, served in a bright blue store you can't miss.

  • 16 May 2026 · Grand openingOur first outlet opens on Bypass Road, Madurai.
