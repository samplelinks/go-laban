From Dubai's dessert craze to your street

Go Laban started with one idea: the creamy, layered desserts everyone was queuing for in the UAE shouldn't need a flight to Dubai.

Our story

Born in the UAE. Made in Madurai.

We brought home the recipes, the Nutella, Lotus and pistachio combinations and the golden kunafa crunch — and built a bright blue store you can't miss.

What we stand for

Every bowl, every time

UAE-authentic

Recipes and layering inspired by the dessert shops of Dubai — the real viral thing, not a look-alike.

Real ingredients

Nutella, Lotus Biscoff, real pistachio, fresh strawberries and crisp kunafa — made fresh and served fresh.

Made to share

Big bowls, gift boxes and a store built for families, friends and the little ones who want dessert first.

The walls

Hand-painted, drip by drip

Our murals were painted by hand, right inside the store — a little Dubai, a little Madurai, a lot of blue.

Meet the gang

One spoon and everyone smiles

From the cool uncle to the little ones, Go Laban is for the whole family.
